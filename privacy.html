Posey — Privacy

Posey is a personal tool: an Android camera that overlays pose guides, and this web console for managing those guides. It is operated by its author for their own use.

Google account data

  • Posey asks for your email address (to show who is signed in) and the drive.file permission.
  • drive.file lets Posey see and change only the files Posey itself creates — a Posey folder holding pose images and their analysis JSON. It cannot read anything else in your Drive.
  • Nothing is stored on any Posey server — there isn't one. Data lives in your Google Drive and on your own devices.

Images and analysis

When you ask Posey to analyse a pose, a downscaled copy of that image is sent to the Anthropic Claude API using your own API key, which stays on your device or in your browser and is never synced. Anthropic's terms govern that request.

Removing access

Revoke Posey at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the Posey folder from your Drive.
